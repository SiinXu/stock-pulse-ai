# 04 Market review: market mood before single names

Market review answers **how the whole market feels**, not whether one stock is a buy.

> Research only — **not investment advice**.

## Open

**Research → Market review**, `/research/market`, palette “market/review”. Auto-run deep link: `?action=run`—use clean URL when you only want history.

## When

After the close (best), intraday as a thermometer (respect incomplete bars), or open history instead of re-running daily.

## Steps

Trigger review → wait for completion → open newest history row → read indices → breadth → sectors → risks/quality → pick at most 1–2 themes → Workbench on specific codes.

## Misreads to avoid

Index up ≠ your holding up. Hot sector ≠ every name is chaseable. Incomplete daily bar → discount bravado.
